The release timeline

Every release, newest at the bottom — the line the firmware has actually walked, from a booting Slint shell to a self-updating fleet of two.

v0.2.0Slint UI shell

The 5-page carousel, launcher, AOD, and the Mesh Familiar — on-demand framebuffer, SMOLv1 mesh, six games, weather, pedometer, BLE, the OTA A/B layout, and defmt-rtt debug.

v0.3.0Migration tail — AOD, the Familiar & a half-res framebuffer

The headline fix: games and Settings launch in any radio state, because the app framebuffer dropped to half resolution — 205×251 RGB332 (~51 KB) instead of ~201 KB, upscaled 2× on flush, with apps still drawing at full 410×502. The full-res buffer simply could not share the C6's single SRAM region with a resident Slint scene plus the radio stacks. Also: AOD rendered by the shell, the Mesh Familiar status cluster + gyro parallax on the clock, an LP-core power row, and the mesh radio decoupled from WiFi credentials (ESP-NOW needs only the STA PHY, not an association).

v0.3.1On-glass fixes

WiFi actually works: the toggle stopped dropping taps (a debounce window was silently eating a WIFI tap within 1 s of the idle check) and now toasts “No WiFi credentials — set in Settings” instead of silently no-opping. The WIFI / BLE / MESH hit areas grew 66×44 → 78×64 (+72%) to be reliably finger-tappable, and the Sensors page shows step count.

v0.4.0Sleep, sensors & testable crates

Light-sleep AOD, the WLED WiZmote remote, an RSSI treasure hunt, a home-energy screen, die-temperature, and host-tested no_std workspace crates.

v0.5.0Home Assistant climate & live energy

Real thermostats, driven from the wrist: a bidirectional MQTT climate session behind a Climate list screen plus a per-device detail overlay (setpoint steppers, mode picker), with crates/climate-model as the pure no_std state core — host-tested against golden vectors, including panic-safety on untrusted device names. The home-energy screen went live on real battery/solar/grid values, fed by Node-RED bridge flows. Under the hood, the main heap was trimmed 240 KB → 228 KB to grow the stack: on the C6 the stack is whatever the linker leaves between _bss_end and _stack_start, so shrinking the heap is what grows it.

v0.5.1Stack-floor guardrail & climate polish

A boot-time check on the stack gap, so a future heap bump can't silently eat the stack again. Setpoints apply optimistically and flush any unsent change when the screen closes; Energy gates on a live connection instead of rendering -1 placeholders; and a shared BackChevron + 72 px setpoint steppers brought uniform 78×64 back targets across climate, energy, wled, and hunt.

v0.6.0Voice & audio arrive

Voice push-to-talk over a LAN STT gateway, speaker playback, a wave of touch-responsiveness + launcher fixes, and the AUDIO launcher section.

v0.7.0The mic, themes & Home Assistant

The working mic (#7 — mics live on a separate ES7210 ADC; driver + ALDO1 rail), real on-glass speech-to-text, mic gain control, the plugin/app registry, the 4-scheme theme system, and the esp32c6_watch Home Assistant component (climate/energy + a media_player speaker queue).

v0.8.0Touch feedback & the paged launcher

The touch-feedback overhaul (shared controls.slint, bold pressed states on ~52 targets, ≥44 px hit areas), the paged 3×3 launcher, partial rendering v2 (#18), wrist-raise wake, an IMU step-counter fix, an AXP2101 charger profile, the UI test automator, and the CO5300 panel confirmed (#17).

v0.8.1AOD never-panic

The AOD light-sleep panic fix (#43: esp-hal RC_FAST calibration ticks + a never-panic gate) — caught in the field by the second watch.

v0.8.2Aurora wake gesture hints

Duo-tone aurora wake gesture hints — a soft teal-and-blue edge sweep that shows which way the gestures go, then fades as you touch. Reduced-motion aware, tuned per theme.

v0.8.3Reliable zero-touch OTA

Zero-touch OTA made dependable (#25, validated end-to-end): pull and push (retained MQTT announce + monotonic build gate, tools/ota_push.sh), retry that survives link drops, and mesh suppression during updates.

v0.8.4The Fleet

Per-device sigil identity (#34) from the efuse MAC: MAC-derived mesh node ids, per-watch MQTT client ids + OTA topics (ota_push.sh --target <sigil>), and sigil BLE advertising. The first release to land on both watches fully zero-touch over the air.

v0.8.5Sound is back & a stable BLE identity

Speaker SFX restored via the shared I²S TX playback seam (#23) — audio_out::play_pcm() substitutes samples into the always-running silent-clock ring so the mic's clock master never stops; Snake beep + UI tap-clicks return. Plus a stable static-random BLE address per watch from the efuse MAC (#47) and a persistent BLE toggle in config v4 (#46) — HA/Bermuda room-tracking now survives reboots.

v0.8.7Room-aware lights

The Lights plugin (#39): a hero button → MQTT command → HA resolves the watch's Bermuda room and toggles that room, with retained state back to the tile. Plus a hotfix — light-sleep with the BLE controller active could lock the chip, so BLE-on now tick-idles AOD (continuous adverts, so room presence keeps working).

v0.8.8The fastpath release

Five stacked latency + freeze causes, run down by forensics: MQTT state arrivals wake the render loop (was a +1 s idle tick), “Finding your room” stops paying a 10 s backoff racing DHCP, presses while disconnected reject with a hint instead of replaying later, Energy only reads unreachable on a real offline LWT, and the config record is dual-slot mirrored so a freeze can't wipe creds / theme / radio state.

v0.9.0Touch sounds & the Settings hub

Every tap answers: one hoisted hook plays a 12 ms tick for both input families — the Slint shell and the framebuffer apps — at texture volume, never per-widget, with a persisted toggle (#49). The Settings tile became a scene-resident hub of five paged sections, retiring the framebuffer Settings app and the T9 keyboard; joining WiFi is now a scan-based picker with a 4-layer QWERTY keyboard. Config record v5 completed the persistence migration (#46) — mesh, WiFi intent, touch sound and mic gain all survive a reboot. Paid for in ROM: ~397 KB of glyph-set consolidation to fit the slot, behind a new image-size gate.

v0.9.1The channel pin yields

The ch6 ESP-NOW mesh pin was firing between WiFi association attempts, dropping auth frames on other channels — so any watch with MESH persisted on struggled to associate at all. The pin now yields whenever WiFi is actively wanted. Also: the mesh startup log had a hardcoded node id and had been lying about which id it ran with.

v0.10.0Dead AP, live UI — the realtime release

The render loop no longer owns the radio. WiFi, OTA and scanning moved into a dedicated net_task that exclusively holds the controller (#53) — commands in, a snapshot out, a hold mask replacing the old per-tick re-raise scramble, and 2s/10s/60s/300s backoff. The number that matters, measured on glass while the access point was dead: worst frame 202 ms, arm max 135 ms. The same outage used to freeze the watch for 15 seconds. A loop-budget rule — more than 10 ms of blocking in any arm is a bug — plus a per-arm watchdog keep it there, and OTA survives a mid-download reconnect.

Riding along: the edge-gesture shell — bottom-swipe launcher (#29), bottom-hold app switcher with suspend/resume/kill and a badge (#31), top-swipe notification shade fed by MQTT and system events (#32); a power-button SHUTDOWN/REBOOT menu with the 4 s hardware failsafe intact (#48); a 12-band FFT spectrum analyzer (#30); and the OTA slots grown 4 MB → 6 MB (#50) after the margin fell to 5.4 KB — a cable event on both watches, which reset the config records once.

v0.10.1The safety release

A watch bricked itself, and the reason is worth knowing. OTA slot selection trusted otadata — which records a boot request, not a boot fact. Stale otadata left over from the pre-6 MB layout made “the other slot” resolve to the partition the CPU was executing from, so a retained push announce triggered a zero-touch self-overwrite: each 4 KB chunk rewrote the live image until the erase of the sector holding in-use WiFi rodata killed the app mid-write (#55). The running slot now comes from the MMU — the physical flash page the CPU actually executes from — and every flash write is range-checked against a deny-list, with the range math host-tested against the exact incident vectors. Both watches recovered and immunised.

Alongside it: overlays that swallow taps so the chrome beneath is never hit-testable (#54), a two-pass WiFi scan because esp-radio's 10–20 ms dwell is shorter than a beacon interval and misses APs that are right there (#56), and firmware WiFi roaming (#57). That last one closed a two-day mystery: esp-radio has no 802.11r, and its default fast-scan associates with the first matching AP it hears — routinely a distant one whose handshake times out while a strong AP sits beside you. The watch now pins the strongest BSSID itself and reassociates when the link goes weak. Paired with unplugging a USB3 hub that was jamming 2.4 GHz and reseating a loose antenna, AuthenticationExpired went to zero.

v0.11.0The fleet greets by name

Press the hero button and the other watch knows. Additive mesh PING/PINGACK frames carry a sigil-identified greeting (#35); the receiver wakes from always-on, blooms a full-screen accent-ring pulse with the sender's name, and plays a rising two-tone chime — or, if the panel is off or a game holds the framebuffer, chimes and drops a card in the notification shade. Delivery is confirmed, and silence times out honestly at 2 s. Voice also stopped asking twice: an early push-to-talk press now latches and fires itself the moment the link lands, cancelling cleanly if you lift your finger first (#22). And mythic-throne took this release over the air, zero-touch — the first such self-update since the v0.10.1 flash-safety fix, and the payoff for the network task, the slot guard and the roaming all landing together.

v0.12.0Unmissable

A ping you cannot miss (#58). The chime became a four-note rising major arpeggio — C5→E5→G5→C6, the top note held as the arrival — host-tested for pop-free edges and for strictly-rising note order. The pulse now lands over framebuffer games too: a ping suspends the running app through the session manager, frees the ~51 KB framebuffer so the Slint pulse can composite, then re-launches it exactly where it was on dismiss. A fully dark panel wakes for it. And every ping leaves an RTC-stamped card in the shade, so there's a record after the pulse fades.

Sound got a volume you control (#59): a 0–15 level plus mute that every chime, beep, click and touch tick honours, a touch HUD that auto-dismisses, and BOOT/POWER short- and long-presses bound to actions you pick in Settings › Buttons — all persisted in config record v6. One nuance worth the words: a short press only acts if the screen was already bright, so a press in the dark just wakes the watch. And Climate and Energy are live again (#60) — the HA component now publishes the retained MQTT topics the firmware actually reads, no reflash required, with the speaker entity restored along the way.

v0.12.1The freezing, curedlatest

Both watches had started freezing, and the cause wasn't in our code at all: esp-radio 0.18's WiFi blob null-dereferences inside ppRxFragmentProc during scan and associate — a deterministic panic about 2.2 seconds into boot, 100% of the time, straight into a reboot loop (#61). The fix is the single RX-aggregation knob 0.18 exposes: disable AMPDU RX at WiFi bring-up. Crash rate went 100% to 0% on both watches, with association, DHCP and roaming intact — cable-flashed, because crash-looping firmware can't update itself over the air. The harness that measured it (tools/watch_soak.py: reset N times, classify every boot, report crash-rate and time-to-crash) is now part of the tooling.

Audio pipeline

5 open

The TX playback seam shipped in v0.8.5 (#23) — and the spectrum analyzer in v0.10.0 (#30) — now climb the rest, from a music player to a full spoken-conversation loop. Bluetooth earbuds are not on that list: the C6 silicon has no ISO link layer, so LE Audio is a permanent no-go here (#62) and classic A2DP never worked either — audio off the wrist goes to room speakers or a custom PCM receiver instead.
