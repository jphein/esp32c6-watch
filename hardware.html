Capabilities

Features

Everything the board exposes — the surface this Rust firmware is built on.

  • 2.06″ round-corner AMOLED — 410×502, true blacks, QSPI-driven with DMA
  • Capacitive multi-touch — FT3168, interrupt-driven
  • Wi-Fi 6 + Bluetooth 5 LE — plus IEEE 802.15.4 (Thread / Zigbee)
  • 6-axis motion — QMI8658 accelerometer + gyroscope, on-chip step counter
  • Real-time clock — PCF85063 with independent timekeeping
  • Audio out — ES8311 codec → class-D speaker amplifier (enable-gated)
  • Voice in — dual-microphone array into the ES8311 ADC (with AEC front-end)
  • Battery + charging — Li-Po connector managed by the AXP2101 PMIC
  • USB-C — native USB-Serial-JTAG (flash, log & debug over one cable)
  • Expansion pads — VBUS, 3V3, BAT, UART (TX/RX) and spare GPIO
  • Buttons — BOOT (GPIO9) and PWR / reset
  • OTA-ready flash layout — factory + OTA partitions on the 16 MB flash

Audio subsystem (the current work): the two MEMS microphones feed the ES8311 codec's analog ADC (there is no separate digital-mic ADC on the board). Capture is ES8311 ADC → I²S RX → DMA. The speaker path is I²S TX → ES8311 DAC → class-D amp, gated by the GPIO6 enable line.
